# Frozen scientific scope

This file is the stop contract for the F2 release. Release maintenance may
correct facts, wording, packaging, citations and reproducibility, but it may
not silently replace the scientific thesis.

## Central claim

In a closed two-candidate task on one fixed 54-speaker VCTK complement, clones
retain an event-linked signal that identifies which same-speaker conditioning
event was used even when prompt and candidates use the two different recorded
pickup paths. The result is large under one fixed ECAPA readout and has the
same aggregate direction under one fixed WavLM-SV readout and after swapping
microphone direction.

The single permitted upgrade reuses the complete clone set in a stricter
clone-to-clone comparison. Query and candidates use different cloning systems,
different output texts and opposite prompt microphones. The event-linked
direction remains material under both fixed SV readouts in both microphone
directions. This removes real candidate audio, same-generator, same-output-text
and shared-generation-seed explanations for that extension.

This does not isolate a physical carrier. Candidate-event transcript, prosody,
articulation, background/room state and their model interactions remain bundled.
It also does not establish open-set presence detection, population inference,
an independent replication, or a universal representation effect.

## Fixed evidence and provenance boundary

- The complete 3,456-clone A/B crossover and its 54-speaker aggregation are
  fixed; no system, text, speaker or direction is selected by outcome.
- EXP-206 uses all 31,104 predeclared cross-generator/cross-text comparisons;
  same-system, same-text and subgroup analyses cannot rescue its four-cell
  intersection-union verdict.
- The evaluation roster is disjoint from the two development tiers but is their
  complement and therefore inherits ECAPA-informed composition. Within-roster
  A/B pair selection is metadata-only and is publicly reconstructible.
- The analysis plan and execution records are contemporaneous internal project
  history without an independently verifiable public timestamp. The release
  does not call them externally preregistered.
- The public artifact reproduces the score census, statistics and verdict and
  publishes the original trust-root hashes. It does not prove end-to-end audio
  generation merely from score/receipt consistency.
- The released execution source contains documented portability adaptations.
  A fresh Level-3 run creates a new execution config and new hashes; it is not
  represented as the historical sealed run.

## Completion and stop rule

The paper closes when its claims match the boundary above, selection
ancestry is publicly verifiable, Level-1 gates and PDF build pass in a clean
clone, and a read-only regression check finds no material defect against this
contract. EXP-206 is the one explicit upgrade. No further scientific expansion
enters this release unless a new reproducible fatal error is found.
